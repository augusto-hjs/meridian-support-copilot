# Reset your password

**If you know your current password**, go to *Settings → Security → Change password*.

**If you're locked out:**
1. Go to the sign-in page and click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address on your account.
3. We email a reset link that is valid for 60 minutes.
4. Follow the link and choose a new password (minimum 10 characters, one number, one symbol).

**Didn't get the email?** Check spam, confirm you used the right address, and make sure `no-reply@meridian.example` is allow-listed. Reset links expire after 60 minutes — request a new one if needed.

**Single sign-on (SSO).** If your workspace uses Google or SAML SSO, Meridian does not store a password for you. Reset it with your identity provider instead. Workspace owners can toggle SSO in *Settings → Security → SSO* (Business plan).

**Two-factor authentication (2FA).** If you lost your 2FA device, a workspace owner can reset 2FA for you from *Settings → Members*. If you are the only owner and are locked out, contact support to verify ownership.
