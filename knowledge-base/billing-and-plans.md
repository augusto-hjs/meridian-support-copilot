# Billing and plans

Meridian offers three plans, billed monthly or annually (annual saves ~17%).

| Plan     | Price (monthly) | Seats        | Projects  | Analytics history |
|----------|-----------------|--------------|-----------|-------------------|
| Free     | $0              | Up to 3      | 2         | 7 days            |
| Pro      | $12 / seat      | Up to 25     | Unlimited | 12 months         |
| Business | $22 / seat      | Unlimited    | Unlimited | Unlimited         |

**Changing plans.** Go to *Settings → Billing → Change plan*. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle.

**Payment methods.** We accept all major credit cards and, on the Business plan, invoicing via bank transfer (net-30). Payment is processed by Stripe; Meridian never stores full card numbers.

**Taxes.** Prices exclude VAT/GST, which is added at checkout based on your billing country.

**Failed payments.** If a charge fails, we retry three times over seven days. Your workspace stays active during this window. After that, it moves to read-only until payment succeeds.

For plan cancellation and refunds, see "Cancel subscription and refunds".
