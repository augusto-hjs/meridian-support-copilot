# Invite team members and roles

**Inviting people.** Go to *Settings → Members → Invite*. Enter email addresses (comma-separated for bulk) and pick a role. Invites expire after 14 days.

**Roles:**
- **Owner** — full control, including billing and workspace deletion. There must always be at least one owner.
- **Admin** — manages members, projects, and integrations; cannot change billing.
- **Member** — creates and edits projects they're added to.
- **Guest** — read-only access to specific projects; guests don't count toward paid seats on the Business plan.

**Seats and billing.** On Pro and Business, each Owner/Admin/Member occupies a paid seat. Adding a member mid-cycle is prorated. Removing a member frees the seat immediately; the credit applies to your next invoice.

**Transferring ownership.** An owner can promote another member to Owner in *Settings → Members*, then optionally downgrade themselves.

**SCIM provisioning** is available on Business for automated user management via your identity provider.
