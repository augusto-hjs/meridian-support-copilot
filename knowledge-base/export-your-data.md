# Export your data

**Project export.** Open a project → *⋯ menu → Export*. Choose CSV (tasks and fields) or JSON (full structure including comments and history). Large exports are prepared asynchronously; we email a download link when ready. Links expire after 24 hours.

**Workspace export.** Owners can export the entire workspace in *Settings → Data → Export workspace*. This produces a ZIP of JSON files, one per project, plus a members and activity log.

**Analytics export.** Dashboards can be exported to CSV or PNG from the dashboard's *Share → Export* menu.

**Data retention.** Deleted projects go to Trash and are recoverable for 30 days, after which they're permanently removed. On workspace deletion, all data is permanently erased within 30 days, per our privacy policy.

**GDPR / data requests.** To request a copy or deletion of personal data, a workspace owner can file a request in *Settings → Data → Privacy requests*. We fulfill verified requests within 30 days.

**Automated backups.** Business-plan workspaces can schedule weekly workspace exports to a connected Google Drive or S3 bucket.
