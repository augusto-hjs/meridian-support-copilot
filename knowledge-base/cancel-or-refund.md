# Cancel subscription and refunds

**Canceling.** A workspace owner can cancel in *Settings → Billing → Cancel plan*. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period — you keep paid features until then, and you're not charged again. Your data is retained and the workspace drops to the Free plan at period end.

**Refunds.** Meridian offers a **14-day money-back guarantee** on the first purchase of a Pro or Business plan. Within 14 days of the initial charge, contact support for a full refund.

Outside the 14-day window:
- **Monthly plans** are non-refundable for the current period, but you can cancel to stop future charges.
- **Annual plans** may be eligible for a prorated refund of unused full months, at Meridian's discretion, minus any discount already applied.

**Duplicate or accidental charges** are always refunded in full — contact support with the invoice number.

**How to request a refund.** Support cannot process refunds automatically; each refund is reviewed by the billing team. When a customer asks for a refund, open a billing ticket with the account email and invoice number so the billing team can verify and process it. Refunds return to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
